# ApexCart E-Commerce Policies & Frequently Asked Questions

## 1. Orders, Shipping & Delivery
### 1.1 Standard Shipping Timelines
- Standard Ground Shipping takes 3 to 5 business days across the continental US.
- Express Shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.
- International Shipping takes 7 to 14 business days depending on customs processing.

### 1.2 Delay Policy & Compensation
- If a package is delayed by more than 5 business days past the estimated delivery date due to courier logistics:
  - Customers are eligible for a **$10 Store Credit** as compensation.
  - If a package is lost in transit (no tracking update for > 10 consecutive days), a free replacement order or 100% full refund is issued immediately.

---

## 2. Returns & Return Eligibility
### 2.1 Return Window
- Items can be returned within **30 days** of verified delivery date.
- Items requested for return between 31 to 45 days after delivery are eligible for store credit only, subject to management approval.
- Returns requested after 45 days are strictly ineligible.

### 2.2 Item Condition Requirements
- Products must be in original condition, unwashed, unworn, with all tags intact and in original packaging.
- Electronics must include all initial accessories, power cables, and original box with intact serial number tags.

### 2.3 Non-Returnable Items
- Custom/personalized merchandise, perishable goods, downloadable software products, opened hygiene/beauty items, and final-sale clearance items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned.

### 2.4 Return Shipping Fees
- Standard returns: Free pre-paid return shipping label provided for defective items or order errors caused by ApexCart.
- Change-of-mind returns: A $4.99 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund total unless returned in-store.

---

## 3. Refunds & Payment Processing
### 3.1 Refund Timelines
- Refunds are issued once the returned package is received and inspected at our distribution warehouse (typically 2-3 business days after delivery).
- Funds take **5 to 7 business days** to post back to the original payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Apple Pay).
- If 10 business days have passed since return approval and funds have not arrived, the financial provider trace reference (ARN) will be provided for bank escalation.

### 3.2 Instant Store Credit Option
- Customers who choose Store Credit instead of original payment method receive an additional **$5 Bonus Credit** instantly upon return label scan.

---

## 4. Exchanges & Replacements
- Exchanges for a different size or color of the same item are free of charge.
- Replacement items are shipped via Express Shipping once the return label is scanned by the carrier.

---

## 5. Warranties & Defective Products
### 5.1 Electronics Warranty
- All consumer electronics sold by ApexCart come with a **1-Year Manufacturer Warranty** against technical defects.
- In-warranty repairs or replacements are handled at zero cost to the buyer.

### 5.2 Apparel Warranty
- Apparel items featuring manufacturing flaws (e.g., loose seams, broken zippers within 60 days of purchase) qualify for instant replacement without requiring a return of the damaged item.

---

## 6. Escalations & Manager Intervention Criteria
- Human Agent Handoff / Manager Review is mandatory under any of the following conditions:
  1. Customer exhibits strong negative sentiment, hostility, or explicit request to speak with a human/manager.
  2. Order has been delayed 3 or more times or has multiple recurring delivery failures.
  3. Exception requests for refunds exceeding $200 or outside the standard 30-day window.
  4. Suspected courier theft, damaged package claims without carrier resolution, or address fraud concerns.
